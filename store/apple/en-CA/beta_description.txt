BEACON Mobile is the iOS and Android client for Beacon, the real-time MeshCore packet analyzer. On first launch, add the URL of a Beacon server and live data starts flowing.

What to try:
• The live packet feed, filters and search
• Opening a packet: decoded details, observers and "Map all paths"
• The live map, node and observer pages
• Analytics tabs
• Leaving the app and coming back (the feed should catch up)

Send feedback right from TestFlight: take a screenshot in BEACON or use the TestFlight app. Bugs and ideas are also welcome at github.com/MeshCore-Beacon/beacon-flutter-app/issues.
