New in 2.3.0
- Repeater telemetry (beta): battery, temperature, airtime and counters on repeater pages, Atlas cards and the map.
- Poller view: see which repeaters a poller polls and how many slots it has free.
- Accounts: sign in and your Atlas follows you between the app and the web.
- Alerts by push or email: battery, offline, reboots and more.
- Atlas: new tabs, tags, a 7 day range and an edit mode.
